# Android application surface

Mode: Operate. Code-led native implementation. The user explicitly requests a complete autonomous overhaul with Compose Material 3, an understated visual identity, readable data, a strong dark theme, and no flashy effects. No mockup or raster asset is approved or needed. The context launcher was unavailable; this brief records the supplied constraints and implemented surface.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Recording a daily value should take one field and one clear save action; understanding its change should be equally direct.

**OWN-WORLD:** Native Material 3 controls on quiet warm neutral light surfaces and deep green ink dark surfaces. Teal establishes the primary action. Positive, negative, and unchanged values have readable semantic colors with arrows, signs, and labels. Platform typography supports English and Persian without a decorative display face. No imagery, gradients, ornamental cards, or simulated financial-market data.

**STORY:** Enter a value/date on Balance, inspect recorded changes and their actual dates, correct individual records in History, and control theme/language/files in Settings. Empty and first-entry states explain what is needed before a candle exists.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** App title, latest full balance, stored date and latest change, followed by the real chart. Date/value/Save remain at the bottom within easy reach. History and Settings are available through native navigation. Content scrolls within the available window, including when the keyboard or increased font size reduces space.

**FORM:** Material controls, tabular numeric typography, open vertical sections, body-only candles with a distinct selection, and concise transitions. Phones use a bottom navigation bar; windows at least 600 dp wide use a navigation rail. Native document/date pickers and focused edit/import dialogs protect deliberate changes. The unavailable launcher produced no concept-seed key; the user's explicit native Material direction and autonomous scope govern this implementation.
